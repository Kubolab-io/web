How much you save hiring in Latin America instead of the US

Written by

Juan Rincón, commercial director

Date

January 29, 2025

If you hire in the US, you are often paying more than the role requires. Companies regularly hire in Latin America at a much lower salary without dropping the quality of the work. This piece looks at the cost gap and how the hire actually gets done.

A senior developer in the US can earn $120,000 to $150,000 a year. Someone with similar skills in Latin America often costs $40,000 to $60,000. That gap is why US companies look south.

The savings are only part of it. You also get people who have worked at global companies and startups, in time zones that overlap a US workday, often with strong English.

How to hire in Latin America without opening a local company

International hiring gets messy on contracts, payroll, and benefits. On Kubo, you meet candidates in Colombia, agree on a salary, and we employ them there. You do not open a Colombian entity.

  • You review CVs and salary ranges
  • You run the interviews
  • Kubo signs the employment contract in Colombia
  • Health insurance, workers’ compensation, retirement contributions, and payroll

Hiring in Latin America is a practical way to grow a team without US salary levels, while keeping hours that overlap yours.

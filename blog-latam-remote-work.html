How Latin American talent is changing remote work for US companies

Written by

Christian Quesada, COO

Date

March 17, 2025

Remote work changed how companies build teams. As they look past their own city, Latin America has become a practical place to find strong professionals who can work the same hours as a US team.

US companies hire in Latin America for the skills, the overlap in time zones, and a salary that is lower than a US offer for similar work.

Unlike hiring several time zones away, Latin America sits close to the US workday. You can talk the same afternoon, which is what makes a remote team feel like a team.

Skilled, more affordable, and used to US teams

Software, data, operations, and support roles are all common. Colombia, Mexico, and Argentina produce bilingual professionals who want to work with international companies.

US salaries and benefits are expensive. Latin America is the lower-cost option that still covers the role.

Many of these professionals have already worked for North American companies, so they join an existing team without a long cultural ramp.
